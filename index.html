PocketDeck

An unofficial Android companion for Pokémon TCG Pocket: scan your collection and get competitive decks you can build from the cards you own.

InstallSource on GitHub

Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or DeNA. Despite the similar name, also not affiliated with the GitHub organization PocketDecks, whose card data it uses. Built with AI coding assistance (Claude Code).

Scan

Read your collection straight from the game's My Cards screen, with the scrolling done by you or by an optional auto-scroll (details).

Decks

  • Recent tournament decks, ranked by how well you can build them, with stand-ins for missing cards
  • Decks built from your own cards
  • Opening odds for each deck
  • "What to chase": the cards you lack that would finish the most decks
  • Optional suggestions from an AI service you set up

Import into the game

Every deck comes with the game's import QR code, so you can load it into the game right away.

Everything on your phone

  • Several accounts
  • English and German
  • Light and dark theme
  • Your collection and settings stay on your phone

Install

Android only. Version 1.1.0. Not on the Play Store.

F-Droid updates itself

Add this repository in F-Droid, Droid-ify or Neo Store (or scan the code):

https://codestev.github.io/PocketDeck/repo

The client shows a fingerprint when you add it. It must be:

82 BF A8 99 F2 D1 4D 82 70 65 73 51 9D A5 FC D8 74 49 C0 B9 5F DB B2 AE 60 3A D0 91 47 BB 66 3E

Open in F-Droid

APK update by hand

Download the signed APK and open it. A newer APK installs over the old one and keeps your data.

Download APK

Check it against SHA256SUMS.txt. Older versions are on the releases page.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no server of ours. See the privacy policy for what stays on the phone, what is downloaded and what the AI feature sends.

Data and credits

  • Card data: PocketDecks/pokemon-tcg-pocket-cards (AGPL-3.0-or-later), scraped from Limitless TCG
  • Tournament decks: Limitless TCG
  • Card images and German names: TCGdex
